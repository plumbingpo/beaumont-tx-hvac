The second is a furnace that short-cycles or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Fort Worth HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
