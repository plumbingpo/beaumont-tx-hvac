Food Service Comfort

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Beaumont

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Beaumont HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Beaumont and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
